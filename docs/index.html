A little care goes a long way

Small habits. Healthier smiles.

Simple everyday steps can help protect your teeth and gums between dental visits.

Plan your next check-up →
01
DAILY ROUTINE

Brush gently, twice a day

Use a soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste. Brush for two minutes, reaching every side of each tooth.

02
BETWEEN TEETH

Make flossing a daily habit

Carefully clean between your teeth once a day to help remove plaque where your toothbrush cannot reach.

03
PREVENTIVE CARE

Keep regular dental check-ups

Routine visits help your dentist spot changes early and give you advice tailored to your smile.

04
SMART CHOICES

Choose water more often

Water helps rinse away food after meals. Try to limit frequent sugary drinks and snacks throughout the day.

05
PERSONALIZED ADVICE

Ask about your own smile

Everyone's dental needs are different. Your dentist can recommend the right care for your teeth and gums.
